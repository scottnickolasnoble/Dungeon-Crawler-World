This is everything the game still needs. It covers:

- the Undercity's missing townspeople
- the new faction units
- the rival contestants
- the hidden-club regulars
- the gods and their priests

It was built from `art/WORLD.md`.

**Settings:** same as before (text tab, H3.1 Best Quality, 4K, PBR off, Triangle, 40,000).

**Saving:** `art/incoming/<folder>/<folder>.glb`, then tell Claude "import the new ones". Heights come from `art/tripo_queue_3.json`, so you don't type them.

**Creatures** (no T-pose) won't walk until they're rigged; they're marked with ⚠.
