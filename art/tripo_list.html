Downstairs Casting Sheet

Every monster, boss and NPC for all 18 floors: 167 models. 100 belong on the floors already built, and 67 are for Floors 7 to 18.

How to do it in one go

  1. Make the first three, then stop. Traffic-Cone Goblin, Intern Pip and Sergeant Pylon. Tell Claude, so the look and the credit cost get checked before you spend on the rest.
  2. For each one, tap Copy prompt, paste it into Tripo, and use text to model at the highest texture quality, with T-pose on if it's offered.
  3. Download as .glb. For anything marked Humanoid, also get .fbx, because Mixamo needs it.
  4. Save to art/incoming/ in a folder with the name shown, then tick it off. Your ticks stay on this device.
