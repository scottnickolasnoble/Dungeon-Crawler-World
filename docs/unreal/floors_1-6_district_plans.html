Floors 1–6: district plans

Each floor is a hub you can always run back to, four or five districts around it, and two boss chambers built as their own small levels. Districts connect through ramps, doors, lifts and bridges, with a short load or seamless streaming. It's a first pass: correct anything that's off.

How the pieces fit in Unreal. Each district is its own map (L_F1_LevelB, and so on). The hub is the "persistent" level, and districts load in through level streaming when you walk through their connection, which feels seamless. Or use one World Partition map per floor if it runs well on your PC. Boss chambers are always separate small maps entered through their gate, so each one can be fully hand-built and hand-lit. Same scale rules as Floor 0: 1 m = 100 units.
