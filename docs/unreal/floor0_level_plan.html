Floor 0: The Landing

The Network's Orientation Centre. Bureaucratic, bright, wrong: a TV-studio lobby that's a little too eager. It teaches every system the game uses, plants the first seed of the mystery, and ends with the lights going out behind you.

Footprint≈ 70 × 150 m
Main path≈ 260 m walked
Play time15–25 min
Zones9, one optional
EnemiesCardboard goblins only

The map

Top-down, not to exact scale. The numbers are the order the player goes through. Gates (▬) stay shut until the zone before is done.

The zones, in order

1

The Arrival Stairwell

≈ 6 × 10 m · ceiling 3 m · two flights down
Walking · looking

The last bit of Earth: a beige apartment stairwell. You come down in your bathrobe because the telly told you to. At the bottom, a heavy door with a bright Network logo that doesn't belong.

Props
Concrete stairs, handrail, fire extinguisher, takeaway menus on the floor, a flickering tube light, the Network door.
Light
One sickly, flickering fluorescent tube (cool white). Dim and ordinary, so the next room hits hard.
Sound
A TV playing upstairs, muffled; the tube buzzing; your footsteps.
Blueprint
BP_FlickerLight; the door is an auto-opening BP_Door.
2

The Welcome Atrium

≈ 30 × 24 m · ceiling 9 m · balcony at 5 m
First impression · the show

The door opens onto blinding brightness: a double-height TV lobby, too clean, too cheerful. A giant TV wall shows the Host welcoming "Season 47". Queue barriers snake towards the desk. Six to ten other new arrivals in pyjamas mill about, confused. Camera drones drift overhead. A balcony runs round the top where no one ever goes.

Props
TV wall (12 m wide), queue stanchions, potted plastic plants, welcome banners, leaflet racks, a confetti cannon that fires once as you enter, camera drones, bins full of bathrobes.
Light
Hard white studio key lights (5600 K), a magenta and teal accent wash on the walls, spotlights picking out the desk. Over-bright on purpose: push exposure up a touch in this room's Post Process Volume.
Sound
Upbeat lobby muzak, PA announcements ("Please have your face ready"), a studio audience laughing at nothing.
Blueprint
BP_TVScreen (plays a video with a Media Texture), BP_NPC_Contestant (idle and wander), BP_CameraDrone (drifts and turns to look at you).

PA: "Welcome, contestant! Please proceed to the Orientation Desk. Smiling is mandatory. Smiling is being monitored."

3

The Orientation Desk

At the far end of the atrium · curved desk ≈ 8 m
Talking · quests · inventory

Intern Pip paces behind a curved reception desk, far too nervous for the job. He starts Orientation, hands you a Welcome Box and the Network Materials Ledger, and points you to the wings. Behind him, a screen loops the orientation video.

Props
Curved desk, stacks of Welcome Boxes, a bell nobody answers, name-badge printer, a "Today's Contestants" counter ticking up.
Light
Two warm spotlights on Pip, so he's the first thing your eye finds.
Blueprint
BP_NPC_Pip (paces, faces you when you talk, dialogue), BP_WelcomeBox (E to open: gives items), BP_TVScreen for the video.
Mystery
The video skips. Looking at the screen at that moment shows one frame of the Builders' symbol. Pip: "It does that sometimes. Anyway!"
4

The Amenities Hall

West wing · ≈ 12 × 11 m plus a 3 m corridor
Water · food · safe rooms · saving

The survival lesson. Drink from the fountain, take a snack, and see a Crash Pad: the safe-room booth you'll rent on every floor, where you sleep, heal and save.

Props
Water fountain (the tutorial step), vending machines (one OUT OF ORDER, one eating coins), a snack table of grey "Nutrient Bars", a Crash Pad booth with a bunk and a TV that won't turn off.
Light
Cool office fluorescents, one buzzing. Less glamorous than the atrium: you're seeing behind the set.
Blueprint
BP_Interactable children: BP_Fountain, BP_Vending, BP_CrashPad (save point).
5

The Development Suite

East wing · ≈ 14 × 12 m
Skill tree · crafting · materials

A "Contestant Development" room in corporate-motivational style. A big touchscreen kiosk opens the skill tree. A workbench teaches crafting with the starter kit: make your first pipe weapon from scrap and cone plastic.

Props
Skill kiosk, crafting bench with vice and tools, a wall chart of materials ("Cone Plastic: RARE?"), motivational posters ("YOUR DEATH IS CONTENT").
Light
Warm desk lamps and a cold glow from the kiosk screen.
Blueprint
BP_SkillKiosk (opens the skill UI), BP_CraftingBench (opens the crafting UI).
6

The Training Yard

≈ 20 × 16 m · ceiling 6 m · gate opens after zones 4 and 5
Light and heavy attacks · stamina · block and parry

A rubber-floored gym. Training dummies teach swinging. A heavy dummy shows stamina. A slow mechanical "parry dummy" swings at you on a timer, with a red warning on the floor, to teach block, parry and dodge before anything can hurt you.

Props
Dummies (one with a Network smile painted on), a heavy bag, the parry dummy (a robot arm with a foam bat), stamina charts, lockers.
Light
Gym high-bay lights: bright, even and functional.
Blueprint
BP_TrainingDummy (takes hits and shows numbers), BP_ParryDummy (telegraphed swing), BP_Gate (opens when the quest step is done).
7

The Practice Arena

Round, ≈ 26 m across · bleachers all round · ceiling 12 m
Real combat · telegraphs · dodging · fans

The set piece. A small round arena with a cheap sports-show feel. The bleachers are full of cardboard cut-out audience members, plus a handful of real, bored spectators. Three waves of cardboard goblins come out of side gates: two, then four, then six with a "Captain" in a party hat. Your first kill puts the viewer count on the scoreboard from 3 up to 11, and Pip celebrates far too much over the PA.

Props
Low arena wall, spawn gates, scoreboard ("VIEWERS: 3"), cardboard audience, tyres and sandbags as cover, a commentator booth where Pip appears.
Light
Stadium rig: four spotlights from above, coloured washes that change per wave, flashing red when a wave starts.
Blueprint
BP_ArenaManager (runs the waves and opens the exit when done), BP_CardboardGoblin (a child of the C++ monster class), BP_Scoreboard.
Note
Drop a NavMesh Bounds Volume over the arena so goblins can path round the cover.

Pip, over the PA: "THAT'S A KILL! On camera! Oh, they're going to LOVE you. Probably. Statistically, some of them."

8

Staff Only (optional, hidden)

Behind the arena · ≈ 4 × 12 m service corridor
Exploring pays · the mystery

A "STAFF ONLY" door behind the bleachers is propped open with a broken chair. Behind it, the Network's set dressing stops. Peeling studio wallpaper shows much older concrete underneath, covered in human scratch tallies, and the Builders' symbol cut into the wall, older than everything around it. A lore page lies on the floor.

Props
Peeling wallpaper decals over old stone, scratch-mark decals, the symbol, cables, a broken chair, a dropped lanyard from a previous season.
Light
One bare bulb, and a red emergency light. The only room that's genuinely dark.
Sound
The lobby music muffled through the wall, and something deep and mechanical humming below.
Blueprint
BP_LorePage (E to read); decals for the scratches.
9

Going Down

≈ 8 × 20 m hall · ceiling dropping from 6 m to 3 m
Leaving · tone shift

The exit hall to the stairs. Pip is waiting at the door and says goodbye like someone who has said it hundreds of times and doesn't expect to see you again. As you walk on, the lights switch off one by one behind you, the cheerful music slows and detunes, and only the green FLOOR 1 sign is left. Through the door: dark stairs going down.

Props
A long hall, a "THANK YOU FOR ORIENTATING" banner, the stair door, the FLOOR 1 sign.
Light
A row of ceiling lights set up in a Blueprint so each one turns off as you pass. The green exit sign is the last light.
Blueprint
BP_LightsOutCorridor (trigger boxes switch the lights off in order), BP_LevelExit (loads Floor 1).

Pip: "Right. Well. This is where I... yeah. Good luck down there. Really. I mean it this time."

The order to build it in

  1. Grey blockout (days 1–3). All nine zones in plain boxes with Modeling Mode, at the sizes above. No art. Walk the whole path in first person, many times.
  2. Lighting on grey (day 4). Light each zone as described, still in grey. If it already feels bright-then-wrong-then-dark, the level works.
  3. The path works (days 5–7). Doors, gates, Pip with placeholder text, the fountain and the dummies. You can play start to finish.
  4. The arena (week 2). Navmesh, cardboard goblins, waves, the scoreboard.
  5. Art pass (week 3+). Swap grey boxes for real models: the Tripo props, a modular wall kit, decals, the TV videos.
  6. Polish. Sound, the confetti, the lights-out corridor, the video glitch, Nigel's lines.
